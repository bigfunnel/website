# Big Funnel

**WhatsApp Campaigns + CRM**

Big Funnel helps sales and marketing teams run WhatsApp campaigns, track every response, and manage every lead from first message to final outcome.

## Official website

[https://bigfunnel.co](https://bigfunnel.co)

## What Big Funnel does today

- WhatsApp campaign creation and audience segmentation
- Reply and conversation tracking
- CRM lead profiles and pipeline stages
- Team ownership, notes, follow-up tasks, and reminders
- Campaign-to-outcome reporting

## Coming next

Instagram, Facebook, Meta Lead Ads, forms, calls, email, and commerce integrations are planned for upcoming releases. They are not represented as currently available features.
